    Private by design

    Configuration only, with browser-native sync

    Your browser may synchronize settings through its own account where supported and enabled. Firefox and Chromium sync separately; Firefox for Android currently keeps extension settings on the device. Free Search Switcher runs no sync server and its developer receives no settings. Search queries and browsing history are never stored or synchronized. A submitted query is sent only to the search engine you choose. There are no analytics, tracking, or Free Search Switcher accounts.
